How the 2026 World Cup Works

First-ever 48-team World Cup, hosted across the USA, Canada and Mexico — 104 matches in total. Here's the path from 48 teams down to one champion.

48
Teams
12 groups (A–L) of 4. Every team plays 3 group games.

Top 2 from each group (24) + the 8 best 3rd-placed teams advance.

Straight knockout — lose and you're out.

Winner lifts the trophy — and their owner takes the €240.
66.7% of teams reach the knockouts (was 50% in 2022) — third place can now be enough to survive.
8 matches for the eventual winner — one more than the old format, because of the extra Round of 32.
Tiebreakers for the group stage: points, goal difference, goals scored, then head-to-head, fair-play points and FIFA ranking.
For the pot: forget winner-takes-all — every match your teams play earns points all tournament long. A knocked-out team simply stops adding to your tally, so nobody is truly out of it until the maths says so.

Format source: UCFB — How the New Group Stage Works
